Privacy notice

How information is used in the ISSSC platform

This platform is operated for the UK Armed Forces Winter Sports Association to administer the Inter Service Snow Sports Championships. It is designed to keep attendance, Protocol, event information and billing data in one controlled system.

Information we collect

Registration may include your name, contact details, Service or organisation, role, attendance dates, travel information, accommodation requests, lift-pass requirements, equipment information and dietary requirements. Staff records may also include operational confirmations, billing information and access roles. If you opt in to event notifications, the platform also stores the browser push-subscription endpoint and technical keys needed to deliver notifications to that device.

Why it is used

Information is used to administer invitations and attendance, organise accommodation and transfers, support event operations, provide attendee information, manage sponsorship and produce accurate event billing. Dietary and other sensitive information is restricted to staff who need it for event delivery.

Event notifications

Push notifications are optional. Your browser will ask for permission before they are enabled. The subscription is linked to your signed-in account only for delivery of selected ISSSC event updates, and you can turn notifications off from My trip at any time.

Access and sharing

Attendees can only access their own confirmed personal event record. Staff access is role-based. Information is not made public unless it is deliberately published as public event content, such as a public biography, programme item or sponsor listing. Attendee-only documents, table plans and personal seating information require authenticated access; private files are delivered with short-lived links.

Security and retention

The application uses authenticated access, database row-level security and server-side privileged workflows. Canonical operational and financial records cannot be changed directly by the browser. Private attendee files are held in non-public storage. Records are retained only for as long as required for event administration, financial, audit and legal obligations; the event authority must set the detailed retention policy. Retired integrations are blocked from public access.

Your information

If you need to correct your information or make a privacy request, contact the ISSSC event team using the contact details supplied with your invitation. Operational changes after registration are managed by the Protocol Team so that the event record remains controlled.
