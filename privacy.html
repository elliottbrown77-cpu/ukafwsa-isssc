Privacy notice

How information is used in the ISSSC platform

This platform is operated for the UK Armed Forces Winter Sports Association to administer the Inter Service Snow Sports Championships. It is designed to keep attendance, Protocol, event information and billing data in one controlled system.

Information we collect

Registration may include your name, contact details, Service or organisation, role, attendance dates, travel information, accommodation requests, lift-pass requirements, equipment information and dietary requirements. Staff records may also include operational confirmations, billing information and access roles.

Why it is used

Information is used to administer invitations and attendance, organise accommodation and transfers, support event operations, provide attendee information, manage sponsorship and produce accurate event billing. Dietary and other sensitive information is restricted to staff who need it for event delivery.

Access and sharing

Attendees can only access their own confirmed personal event record. Staff access is role-based. Information is not made public unless it is deliberately published as event content, such as a biography, programme item or sponsor listing.

Security and retention

The application uses authenticated access and database row-level security. Records are retained only for as long as required for event administration, financial, audit and legal obligations. Retired integrations are blocked from public access.

Your information

If you need to correct your information or make a privacy request, contact the ISSSC event team using the contact details supplied with your invitation. Operational changes after registration are managed by the Protocol Team so that the event record remains controlled.
